# VeroSkills

**AI recruiting software for staffing firms and recruiting teams across blue-collar and skilled-trades work.**

[VeroSkills](https://www.veroskills.com/) (`VeroSkills, Inc.`, also known as **VeroHire**) equips staffing firms and recruiting teams with groups of AI agents that handle early recruiting work—candidate sourcing, text and email outreach, preliminary AI interviews, and calendar scheduling—so human recruiters can focus on candidate relationships, client conversations, and hiring decisions.

---

## How VeroSkills Works

1. **[AI Candidate Sourcing](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-sourcing)** — Searches across job boards, professional networks, and existing ATS records to surface people whose hands-on trade experience matches the open role.
2. **[Text & Email Outreach](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/outreach-scheduling)** — Reaches out by SMS and email to check interest and availability, following up automatically and booking interviews when candidates are free.
3. **[Preliminary AI Interviews](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/ai-interviews)** — Runs structured voice and video conversations around actual job duties, trade certifications, equipment experience, and shift requirements.
4. **[Recruiter Handoff](https://www.veroskills.com/platform/recruiter-handoff)** — Organizes each candidate's work history, interview summary, notes, and full recording so the human recruiter is prepared to lead the next step.
